Our tours

From the historic center of Santiago to the snow of the Andes and the Pacific coast, each tour is guided in Portuguese. Use the filters to find the right tour for you, and save your favorites to compare them later.

Good to know

  • The City Tour is a walking tour that starts at the San Francisco Church. Tours outside the city include hotel pickup.
  • Snow at Farellones and Portillo is most common between June and September.
  • Ski equipment and clothing rental are not included, but the guide can recommend trusted rental shops on the way.
  • Mountain tours depend on road conditions and may be rescheduled by the road authority on snowy days.
