Divisor Turismo

Website Planning Document - WDD131

Site Name

Divisor Turismo

Optional domain availability: divisorturismo.cl

I chose this name because it is my real, existing tour brand. "Divisor" refers to the Andes as the natural divide between Chile and Argentina, which is the backdrop for several of my tours, and it already carries the brand slogan "seu roteiro, nossa história" (your itinerary, our story).

Site Purpose

The site serves as an informational hub for Brazilian tourists visiting Santiago, Chile. It presents the available guided tour packages, explains what each tour includes, and provides a contact form so visitors can request a custom quote or booking.

Scenarios

  • A family planning a 3-day trip to Santiago wants to know which tours are available and what each one includes.
  • A visitor interested in a ski trip wants to know whether the Farellones or Portillo tour includes transportation and equipment rental information.
  • A traveler wants to request a personalized itinerary and needs to know how to contact the guide directly.

Color Schema

The palette is taken from my existing Divisor Turismo brand kit.

Deep Blue #16243D Headings, header background, footer background
Gold #C9A66B Accents, buttons, links, section underlines
Cream #F6F1E7 Page background
Wine #5C1B2E Subheadings, call-to-action highlights

Typography

Two fonts are used: one for headings and one for body text.

Fraunces - used for the page title and section headings

Work Sans - used for body text, lists, and navigation, chosen for its clean, highly legible look on both mobile and desktop screens.
